## About Campus Recall

Campus Recall is a free personal information-retrieval student project by Raj Modi, Chaitanya and Nakshatra for CS437. It is not an official IIT Gandhinagar service and does not imply institutional endorsement.

## Your imported material

You retain ownership of material you import. You are responsible for having permission to store and use it, including content involving other people. Keep exported archives private. Do not use the app to misuse confidential information or infringe others' rights.

## Check the original

Search uses local models and similarity calculations to return saved originals. It does not generate factual answers. OCR, transcription, visual matching and ranking can make mistakes or omit information. Open the original and verify important details, especially deadlines or instructions. A search with no strong matches does not establish that an item was never saved.

Audio search covers English speech. Video scenes are sampled at five-second intervals, so a brief scene may not be indexed. The app can retrieve only content you saved. Supported formats and limits are shown on the Add screen and documented in the user guide.

## Your device and backups

Libraries belong to a browser profile on a particular device. There is no automatic synchronization or recovery service. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted, and a device can run out of space. Keep manual backups of important originals. The export ZIP is unencrypted. Persistent storage is best-effort.

Processing requires the app to remain open. Unfinished jobs resume after reopening. Device memory, browser features and compute capacity affect model support and speed. Initial setup needs internet and significant local storage. No paid inference API or API key is required.

Webpage capture and external links use third-party websites, which have their own policies and availability limits. A browser may block capture. This is a student project under active development. Features, compatibility and support may change. Back up your library before installing an update. The application is provided as available, without a promise that every extraction or search succeeds.

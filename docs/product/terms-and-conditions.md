## Project and intended use

Campus Recall is a student project by Raj Modi, Chaitanya, and Nakshatra for the CS437 Vibe Coding Hackathon. This description does not imply official IIT Gandhinagar endorsement or an institutional service.

The intended product is a free personal retrieval tool for deliberately imported images, PDFs, notes, webpage snapshots, and supported short audio/video. It is intended to help you find and open saved sources. It cannot recover a chat or file you never imported. Accounts, automatic synchronization, generated answers, reminders, and task management are outside the planned v1 scope.

## Results and original sources

Planned OCR, transcription, visual matching, and ranking can miss information or return an incorrect match. Automated excerpts and transcripts are not verified human quotations. Similarity scores are ranking signals, not probabilities of correctness. No result does not prove that an item was never saved.

Open and check the original source, including the relevant PDF page or media interval, before relying on dates, instructions, or other details. Video visual search samples frames every five seconds and may miss a brief scene. Audio search concerns English speech, not music or environmental-sound identification. English is the acceptance baseline; handwriting support beyond that baseline is an extension.

The project does not promise complete retrieval, uninterrupted availability, permanent storage, or a particular processing speed. The documented performance targets are not measured results.

## Storage, backups, and third parties

Each device/browser profile has its own local library. Keep backups outside the app and check that they restore. Browser clearing, eviction, profile changes, or device loss can remove access. App-level encryption at rest and encrypted transfer are not implemented. Browser/OS backup behavior is outside the app's control. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) for the intended data handling and unresolved deployment facts.

Explicit website capture and visits involve third-party websites. A saved snapshot may differ from the current website, and the project does not control that site's content or policies. Planned setup also downloads third-party model/runtime assets. Open-source components and model assets retain their respective licenses and notices; this document does not replace them. Final asset sources and notices still require release review.

## Changes and reporting problems

Project updates may change supported behavior, fix errors, or require local reindexing. Review the [user guide](user-guide.md) and project changes, and back up your library before consequential updates.

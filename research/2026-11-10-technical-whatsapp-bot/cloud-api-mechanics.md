## WhatsApp Cloud API mechanics (official Meta-hosted platform)

All sources accessed 2026-11-10. Meta has moved its WhatsApp documentation from `developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/...` to `developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/...`. Both paths still serve content, and the two sometimes disagree on small details, which are flagged below.

### 1. Getting a working number as a 3-student team with no registered company

According to [Meta Get Started](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/get-started.md) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Medium, tier: Primary), a developer needs a Facebook account or managed Meta account, registration as a Meta developer, a Meta app created with the WhatsApp use case, a business portfolio (an existing one or one created during the flow), and a WhatsApp Business account ("Messaging account") linked to the app. The guide says only that publishing requirements are listed and "you may not have any at this point." It does not make business verification a step. [Meta Business Phone Numbers](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/business-phone-numbers/phone-numbers) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Medium, tier: Primary) adds that completing Get Started automatically creates and registers a test business phone number. This suggests a student team can get to a working test setup with nothing more than personal Facebook accounts and a self-created business portfolio.

**Test number recipients.** Meta's older Get Started page, as indexed by search, says you can add recipients "up to 5 in total," and each recipient must enter a confirmation code sent to them in WhatsApp ([Meta Get Started, legacy path](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/get-started/), accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Medium, tier: Primary). The current rendered page no longer shows that sentence, so it could not be verified directly. [WANotifier help](https://help.wanotifier.com/en/article/test-phone-number-limitations-kt0ly2/) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Low, tier: **Low**) states that "You can not send messages to your customers using a test phone number" and that "Your customers can not send messages to your test phone number." It also says templates created on the test number cannot be reused on a real number. [Wati](https://www.wati.io/en/blog/test-whatsapp-message-sends-safely/) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Low, tier: **Low**) repeats the five-recipient cap. According to [Meta About the Platform](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/about-the-platform) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Medium, tier: Primary), test numbers have "relaxed messaging limits" and need no payment method on file to send templates. The pattern indicates that the test number works for the three developers plus two classmates, but it cannot serve as the public bot for a student cohort. A real number is required for anything beyond five allow-listed testers.

**Is business verification required?** None of the Meta pages fetched makes it a precondition for registering a real number. The documented limits that apply without it are below.

- **Phone numbers.** "New business portfolios are initially capped at two registered business phone numbers." The cap rises to 20 once the business is verified or reaches a 2,000 messaging limit ([Meta Business Phone Numbers](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/business-phone-numbers/phone-numbers), accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Medium, tier: Primary).
- **Messaging limit.** "Newly created business portfolios have a messaging limit of 250." This counts unique users messaged *outside* a customer service window in a moving 24-hour period. The levels are 250, then 2,000, 10,000, 100,000 and unlimited. Business verification is one of three "scaling paths" to 2,000. The other two are partner verification, or sending 2,000 delivered high-quality template messages to unique users outside the window within 30 days. Above 2,000, the limit scales automatically within about 6 hours if quality is high and at least half the current limit was used in the last 7 days ([Meta Messaging Limits](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/messaging-limits), accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: High, tier: Primary). [Clickatell](https://www.clickatell.com/help-center/whatsapp/general-whatsapp/what-messaging-limits-apply-to-my-whatsapp-business-account/) (tier: Established vendor docs) corroborates the 250 starting cap. According to the [Meta Changelog](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/changelog/) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Medium, tier: Primary), on 8 October 2025 limits moved from per phone number to per business portfolio, and the first scaling step rose from 1,000 to 2,000.
- **Display name.** A display name must be supplied at registration. `name_status` can be `AVAILABLE_WITHOUT_REVIEW` or `PENDING_REVIEW`, among other values. The display name is verified automatically "when you reach a higher messaging limit," and it shows at the top of chats and in the chat list only once approved. It can be changed 10 times per 30 days ([Meta Display Names](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/display-names), accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Medium, tier: Primary). [Vonage support](https://api.support.vonage.com/hc/en-us/articles/28026107711516-Why-is-my-WhatsApp-Messaging-Limit-still-at-250-and-Display-Name-not-visible-after-Business-Verification) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Medium, tier: Established vendor docs) confirms that verification alone does not raise the 250 limit or show the display name. The display name review triggers only at the 2,000 tier.
- **Throughput.** Verification "factors into improved functionality, such as higher throughput" ([Meta About the Platform](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/about-the-platform), accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Medium, tier: Primary).

This implies that an unverified student team is fine for Campus Recall. Students message the bot first, so nearly all traffic is replies inside an open customer service window, which by Meta's definition does not count toward the 250 business-initiated limit. The main cost of staying unverified is cosmetic: until approval, students will likely see the raw phone number rather than a "Campus Recall" display name. On verification documents in India, vendor guides ([Zoko](https://www.zoko.io/post/whatsapp-business-api-no-gst-number), [Wati](https://support.wati.io/en/articles/11463208-meta-business-verification-required-documents-by-country), accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Low, tier: **Low**) say GST is common but not strictly required by Meta. Udyam registration, a Shop & Establishment certificate or a business bank statement are alternatives, and the guides disagree on whether Udyam alone suffices. No Meta primary source listing Indian documents was found. A team without a registered entity should plan to remain unverified.

### 2. Receiving: webhooks, media download, sizes, forwarding

**Payload shape.** According to [Meta Webhook Components](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/webhooks/components) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Medium, tier: Primary), every webhook is `object: "whatsapp_business_account"` → `entry[]` → `changes[]`, with `field: "messages"` and a `value` containing `metadata` (`display_phone_number`, `phone_number_id`), `contacts[]` (`profile.name`, `wa_id`, `user_id`) and either a `messages[]` array (inbound) or a `statuses[]` array (sent/delivered/read/failed for outbound). Each message has `from`, `from_user_id`, `id`, `timestamp` and `type`, plus a type-specific object. The per-type reference pages document the following:

- **text:** `text.body`. Links arrive as plain text in the body, and no link-preview metadata is documented ([Meta text webhook](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/webhooks/reference/messages/text)).
- **image:** `image.{id, mime_type, sha256, caption, url}` ([Meta image webhook](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/webhooks/reference/messages/image)).
- **video:** `video.{id, mime_type, sha256, caption, url}` ([Meta video webhook](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/webhooks/reference/messages/video)).
- **document:** `document.{id, mime_type, sha256, filename, caption, url}` ([Meta document webhook](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/webhooks/reference/messages/document)).
- **audio:** `audio.{id, mime_type, sha256, voice, url}`. `voice: true` means the clip was recorded with WhatsApp's voice-note feature, typically `audio/ogg; codecs=opus` ([Meta audio webhook](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/webhooks/reference/messages/audio)).
- **location:** `location.{latitude, longitude, name, address, url}`. Live location is not documented for Cloud API ([Meta location webhook](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/webhooks/reference/messages/location)).
- **contacts:** `contacts[]` with `name`, `phones[]` (including `wa_id`), `emails[]`, `org`, `urls[]`, `addresses[]` and `birthday`. "Many contact properties may be omitted" depending on the user's sharing choices ([Meta contacts webhook](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/webhooks/reference/messages/contacts)).
- **unsupported:** message types Cloud API cannot represent arrive as `type: "unsupported"` with an error. Since 3 November 2025, the webhook shows the actual underlying type ([Meta Changelog](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/changelog/)).

All of the above were accessed 2026-11-10 (confidence: Medium, tier: Primary). Captions are therefore exposed on image, video and document messages, but not on audio. Every inbound media item carries a `sha256`, which Campus Recall can use directly for deduplication.

**Media download.** According to [Meta Media reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/reference/media) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: High, tier: Primary), the flow is: (1) `GET /<MEDIA_ID>` (optionally with `phone_number_id`) to obtain a URL; (2) `GET` that URL with `Authorization: Bearer <token>` ("If you omit your token, the request will fail"); (3) the body is the binary and `Content-Type` gives the MIME type. On a 404, fetch a new URL and retry. The lifetimes are:

- Media URLs "expire after 5 minutes."
- Media IDs received in webhooks expire after **7 days**.
- Media IDs for uploaded media last **30 days**.

The [Meta Changelog](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/changelog/) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: High, tier: Primary) independently records the change: webhook media IDs "stay downloadable for 30 days, dropping to 7 days after October 9 [2025]." It also notes that from 16 October 2025 inbound media webhooks include a `url` property, so step 1 can be skipped where the field is present. This suggests the bot should download each file to Azure Blob within seconds of the webhook rather than storing the media ID for later.

**Maximum sizes.** According to [Meta Media reference](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/reference/media) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Medium, tier: Primary), the supported types and limits are:

| Type | Formats | Max size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audio | AAC, AMR, MP3, M4A, OGG (OPUS mono only) | 16 MB |
| Document | TXT, XLS(X), DOC(X), PPT(X), PDF | 100 MB |
| Image | JPEG, PNG, static WebP; 8-bit RGB/RGBA, ≤4,096 px per edge | 5 MB |
| Sticker | WebP | 100 KB static / 500 KB animated |
| Video | MP4, 3GP; H.264 + AAC only | 16 MB |

The same page states that "the maximum size for media messages on Cloud API is 100 MB" for **inbound** customer media. Larger files produce webhook error **131052** ("Media file size too big. Max file size we currently support: 100MB"), and Meta suggests warning the customer. The page does not label the per-type table as outbound-only, so the reading that the table governs uploads and sends while inbound is capped uniformly at 100 MB is an inference. [360dialog](https://docs.360dialog.com/partner/messaging/media-messages) (via search excerpt, confidence: Low, tier: Established vendor docs) corroborates the 100 MB Cloud API ceiling. This implies that a student forwarding a lecture recording or a large PDF over 100 MB (the consumer app allows much larger documents) will produce an error webhook, not a file, so Campus Recall needs a fallback such as "upload via the web app."

**Forwarded flag.** According to [Meta image webhook](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/webhooks/reference/messages/image), [Meta video webhook](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/webhooks/reference/messages/video) and [Meta text webhook](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/webhooks/reference/messages/text) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Medium, tier: Primary), a `context` object is present when a user forwards a message to the business. `context.forwarded: true` appears if it was forwarded 5 times or fewer, and `context.frequently_forwarded: true` if more than 5 times. The document and audio reference pages fetched do not show `context`. Whether the flag is emitted for forwarded PDFs and voice notes is therefore undocumented, though it is plausible by analogy. No original sender, original timestamp or source chat is exposed.

**Multiple items at once.** No Meta source states how a multi-select forward or a multi-image send is delivered. [n8n community](https://community.n8n.io/t/how-to-handle-multiple-whatsapp-images-in-one-workflow-execution/271976) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Low, tier: **Low**) reports that each image arrives as its own webhook with its own message ID. Twilio's WhatsApp channel likewise splits multi-file sends into one message per file ([Twilio support](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/50592895951387-Missing-Media-Attachments-in-onMessageAdd-and-onMessageAdded-Webhooks), confidence: Low, tier: Established vendor docs). Meta's reference shows `messages` as an array, but every example contains one message. This suggests the bot should treat each item independently and, if grouping matters ("save these 5 photos as one note"), group by sender plus a short time window in its own database.

**Out of axis but relevant: where Meta stores the data.** According to [Meta Local Storage](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/local-storage) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Medium, tier: Primary), message content may be processed internationally for up to 60 minutes. Local Storage then keeps it at rest only in the chosen region, set with `data_localization_region` at `/register`. Meta's own example uses India (`IN`), and [Infobip](https://www.infobip.com/docs/whatsapp/compliance/data-privacy.md) (confidence: Medium, tier: Established vendor docs) also lists India as supported. The changelog adds a "No Storage" option from 1 December 2025. This matters because the project positions itself as private storage, and students' notes will transit Meta's servers before reaching Azure.

### 3. Sending replies

**Customer service window.** According to [Meta Pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: High, tier: Primary), when a user messages or calls, a 24-hour customer service window opens. Each new user message resets it, and non-template messages (text, media, interactive) may be sent only while it is open. Outside the window only pre-approved templates may be sent ([Meta About the Platform](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/about-the-platform), confidence: High, tier: Primary). Sending a free-form message after the window closes returns error **131047**, "More than 24 hours have passed since the recipient last replied" ([Meta Error Codes](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/support/error-codes), accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Medium, tier: Primary).

**Interactive messages.** The limits are as follows:

- **Reply buttons:** up to **3** buttons, titles ≤20 characters, button ID ≤256 characters, body ≤1,024 characters, footer ≤60 characters, and an optional header of text, image, video or document ([Meta reply buttons](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messages/interactive-reply-buttons-messages)).
- **List messages:** up to **10 sections and 10 rows total**, button text ≤20 characters, row title ≤24 characters, row description ≤72 characters, row ID ≤200 characters, body ≤4,096 characters, header and footer ≤60 characters ([Meta list messages](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messages/interactive-list-messages)).
- **CTA URL messages:** a single URL button with label ≤20 characters, body ≤1,024 characters and an optional text, image, video or document header. Tapping it opens the URL in the device browser ([Meta CTA URL](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messages/interactive-cta-url-messages)).

All three were accessed 2026-11-10 (confidence: Medium, tier: Primary). The pattern indicates that a search reply can show up to 10 results in a list, followed by either a document message carrying the original file (by uploaded media ID, ≤100 MB for documents) or a CTA URL button that deep-links into the Campus Recall PWA. URL buttons inside *templates* exist, but the per-template rules were not fetched.

**Templates outside the window.** Templates "generally require approval" and are the only message type allowed outside the window ([Meta About the Platform](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/about-the-platform), confidence: Medium, tier: Primary). For Campus Recall, a template would be needed only for proactive notifications such as a reminder; the core save-and-search loop never leaves the window.

**Pricing (overlaps the pricing axis, included because it changed in the last 6 weeks).** According to [Meta Pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: High, tier: Primary), billing has been per message since 1 July 2025, and user-to-business messages are never charged. Service (non-template, in-window) replies were free from 1 November 2024. **From 1 October 2026, each business phone number gets 1,000 free service messages per month (no rollover), and Meta charges from the 1,001st.** In-window utility templates also became chargeable on that date. [respond.io](https://respond.io/blog/whatsapp-pricing-change-2026) and [YCloud](https://www.ycloud.com/blog/whatsapp-api-message-pricing-update-effective-october-1-2026) (accessed 2026-11-10, tier: **Low** vendor blogs) corroborate the 1,000-message allowance. One vendor quotes about ₹0.145 per service message for India, which is unverified against Meta's rate card (confidence: Low). For India-based billing, accounts of eligible India customers must move to INR by 31 December 2026, and from 1 January 2027 Meta will stop delivering messages from their non-INR accounts. This implies that a pilot with roughly 100 active students exchanging 10+ messages a day would pass 1,000 bot replies a month quickly, and the team would need a payment method attached.

### 4. Throughput, rate limits, webhook delivery and security

**Throughput.** According to [Meta Throughput](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/throughput) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: High, tier: Primary; also stated in [Meta About the Platform](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/about-the-platform)), each number sends up to **80 messages per second** by default. A number is automatically upgraded to 1,000 mps only if its portfolio has an *unlimited* messaging limit, it messages 100,000 or more unique users outside the window within 24 hours, and its quality is Medium or better. Coexistence numbers are fixed at **20 mps**. Exceeding throughput returns **130429**. The per-recipient **pair rate limit** returns **131056** ("Too many messages sent from the sender phone number to the same recipient phone number in a short period of time") ([Meta Error Codes](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/support/error-codes), confidence: Medium, tier: Primary). Meta gives no number. [Wati](https://www.wati.io/en/blog/mcp-server-queue-or-reject-whatsapp-messages/) (confidence: Low, tier: **Low**) cites about 1 message per 6 seconds per recipient, with short bursts tolerated. This suggests the bot should not fire many separate "Saved ✓" acknowledgements when a student forwards 20 files; it should batch them into one reply.

**Webhook delivery.** According to [Meta Create Webhook Endpoint](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/webhooks/create-webhook-endpoint) and [Meta Webhooks Overview](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/webhooks/overview) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: High, tier: Primary), any response other than HTTP 200 triggers retries "immediately, then a few more times … over the next 7 days," "with decreasing frequency." "These retries can result in duplicate webhook notifications." Payloads can be up to **3 MB**, and the endpoint needs a valid CA-signed TLS certificate ("Self-signed certificates are not supported"). mTLS is supported, and Meta recommends it over IP allow-listing because Meta's IPs change. [Hookdeck](https://hookdeck.com/webhooks/platforms/guide-to-whatsapp-webhooks-features-and-best-practices) (confidence: Low, tier: **Low**) also reports the 7-day window. No Meta source states an ordering guarantee or a response timeout. Hookdeck describes delivery as at-least-once with no ordering guarantee, and vendor sources cite a 5–10 second timeout (confidence: Low). The bot should therefore return 200 immediately, enqueue the work, deduplicate on `messages[].id`, and order by `timestamp` rather than by arrival.

**Verification and signatures.** According to [Meta Create Webhook Endpoint](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/webhooks/create-webhook-endpoint) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Medium, tier: Primary), setup sends a GET with `hub.mode=subscribe`, `hub.challenge` and `hub.verify_token`. The server compares the verify token with its stored value and echoes `hub.challenge` with HTTP 200. Each POST carries `X-Hub-Signature-256: sha256=<hex>`, an HMAC-SHA256 of the raw request body keyed with the **app secret**. On a mismatch, the server should treat the payload as invalid and respond with a non-200 status. In Express this means capturing the raw body before JSON parsing.

**Access tokens.** According to [Meta Access Tokens](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/access-tokens) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Medium, tier: Primary), a direct developer accessing its own data should use a **System User access token**. The setup is: create the system user in Business settings, assign the app with "Manage app" (Full control), and give it partial or full access to the WhatsApp account. Then generate a token with `business_management`, `whatsapp_business_management` and `whatsapp_business_messaging`, choosing an expiration preference that can be set to never. User access tokens, including the dashboard's temporary token, "expire quickly, so you have to keep generating a new one every few hours." Meta's current page calls system tokens "long-lived" and does not explicitly use the word "permanent."

### 5. Personal numbers and coexistence

According to [Meta Business Phone Numbers](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/business-phone-numbers/phone-numbers) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: High, tier: Primary), "Numbers already in use with WhatsApp cannot be registered unless they are deleted first." A registered number can still take ordinary calls and SMS but cannot be used in a WhatsApp app. The number must belong to the registrant, include a country code, and be able to receive an SMS or voice OTP. A student's personal WhatsApp number therefore cannot become the bot unless that student deletes their WhatsApp account on it. The practical route is a new prepaid SIM, or a landline that can receive a voice OTP.

**Coexistence.** According to [Meta Coexistence (Onboarding Business App Users)](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/embedded-signup/onboarding-business-app-users) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Medium, tier: Primary), a number on the **WhatsApp Business app** (v2.24.17+, not the consumer app) can be connected to Cloud API while staying active in the app. Messages are mirrored between the two, and app-sent messages arrive as `smb_message_echoes` webhooks. Six months of history can be synced within 24 hours of onboarding. Throughput is fixed at 20 mps. Groups, broadcast lists, disappearing and view-once messages, live location and calls are unsupported. Disconnection follows about 14 days of primary-device inactivity (`PRIMARY_INACTIVITY`) or a move to the consumer app (`BUSINESS_DOWNGRADE`). The onboarding is done through Embedded Signup, and "You must already be a Solution Partner or Tech Provider." The [Meta Changelog](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/changelog/) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: Medium, tier: Primary) records that coexistence launched for solution providers on 11 February 2025, numbers with India's dialling code became eligible on 5 May 2025, and it expanded to the EU, EEA and UK on 23 October 2025. This implies that a student team cannot use coexistence directly as plain developers. They would have to become a Tech Provider (an extra Meta onboarding programme) or onboard through a BSP that supports coexistence. The upside is limited for Campus Recall, so a dedicated new SIM on plain Cloud API is simpler.

### 6. Changes in 2025–2026

- **On-Premises API sunset:** "The final supported version of the On-Premise API client expired on October 23, 2025," and On-Premises can no longer send messages ([Meta On-Premises Sunset](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/on-premises/sunset), accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: High, tier: Primary; corroborated by [Exotel](https://support.exotel.com/support/solutions/articles/3000133444-whatsapp-on-premise-sunset-migration-process), tier: Established vendor docs). Cloud API is now the only official self-integration path, so any tutorial using the Docker-hosted On-Premises client is obsolete.
- **Usernames and BSUIDs:** According to [Meta Business-Scoped User IDs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/business-scoped-user-ids) (accessed 2026-11-10, confidence: High, tier: Primary; corroborated by [WABetaInfo](https://wabetainfo.com/whatsapp-shares-new-username-updates-with-business-partners/), tier: Established), BSUIDs have appeared in webhooks since early April 2026 as `contacts[].user_id` and `messages[].from_user_id`. Their format is a country code, a dot and up to 128 alphanumeric characters, for example `US.13491208655302741918`, and they are scoped per business portfolio. Sending to a BSUID became supported in July 2026, and businesses could reserve usernames from 29 June 2026. If a user adopts a username, `wa_id` and `from` (the phone number) are **omitted** unless the user is in the business's contact book or there was an interaction in the last 30 days. Meta advises: "build your integration to identify users by BSUID." Recent changelog items include a limit of two username changes per 14 days (23 October 2026) and new username error codes (5 November 2026). For Campus Recall this matters directly: the student-to-account mapping in PostgreSQL should key on `user_id` (BSUID), not the phone number.
- **Messaging limits per portfolio** (8 October 2025) and the **webhook media-ID lifetime cut to 7 days** (9 October 2025), both covered above.
- **`url` field on inbound media webhooks:** from 16 October 2025 per the changelog. The per-type reference pages say it rolls out gradually "starting November 12, 2025."
- **Groups API launched** on 6 October 2025, allowing programmatic creation and management of WhatsApp groups ([Meta Changelog](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/changelog/), confidence: Medium, tier: Primary). This could matter if the team wants a class-group bot, but it was not researched further.
- **Service-message charging from 1 October 2026** and **INR billing deadline of 31 December 2026**, covered above.

> Conflicts noted:
> - **Test-number recipient cap:** the "up to 5 in total" sentence appears in Meta's legacy Get Started page as indexed by search, but not in the currently rendered Get Started page. Vendor sources (Low tier) agree on 5.
> - **Messaging tiers:** [Meta Messaging Limits](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/messaging-limits) lists 250, 2K, 10K, 100K and unlimited. [ChakraHQ](https://chakrahq.com/article/whatsapp-api-account-restricted-or-blocked-find-out-why-and-how-to-resolve/amp/) (Low tier) claims Meta is removing the 2K and 10K tiers in Q2 2026. Meta's current page does not reflect that, so Meta's figure is used.
> - **Inbound media `url` field date:** the Meta changelog says 16 October 2025, while Meta's per-type webhook reference pages say a gradual rollout starting 12 November 2025. The code should handle both cases: use `url` if present, otherwise `GET /<media-id>`.
> - **Webhook retry window:** Meta and Hookdeck say 7 days. One forum report cited by search says about 8 hours, and 360dialog says about 24 hours for its own BSP layer. Meta's primary 7-day figure is used.
> - **Coexistence country eligibility:** one older vendor list excludes India. Meta's changelog (5 May 2025) explicitly added India, and Meta's changelog is used.
>
> Gaps:

# Campus Recall product context

Students at IIT Gandhinagar save academic and campus information across files, chats and websites, then forget where they put it. Two documented student interviews support the need to find saved originals and trust that saving succeeded. The full evidence is in SPEC.md and docs/user/.

The job is: save an original, see that it is stored locally, later describe what you remember, and open that exact original at its relevant page or timestamp. This is a personal library, without generated answers, accounts, reminders or remote inference. All core processing runs locally after explicit offline setup. Each device has a separate library with manual ZIP transfer.

The approved audience, input limits, device targets, privacy boundaries, delivery requirements and acceptance statements are in IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md, SPEC.md and docs/implementation/. These are the source of truth. Never invent user evidence or claim physical device validation that has not occurred.
